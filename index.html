How to play

Coworkers email you favors. Each one is a task: compose an email to the right person, pick the right subject, and build the body phrase by phrase. There's no task list — your inbox is all you've got. Ignore a favor for 2 hours and the requester sends an angry follow-up. Anything still undone at 5 PM becomes a citation. Your inbox carries over to the next day. Pay: $250/day + $40 per task − $30 per citation. And beware emails tagged [External Sender] — phishing scams want you to click their links or attachments. Falling for one costs you $50.
